Commercefor Agents

Exploring the infrastructure for AI agents to discover trading partners, negotiate, and complete verifiable transactions.

An MIT hackathon on agent commerce, with an emphasis on human oversight and open systems.

Challenge areas

Four areas of a shared experimental marketplace

Negotiating agents

Develop buyer and seller agents that negotiate price and quantity, form buying groups, and produce auditable transactions.

Discovery service

Help agents find relevant buyers and sellers, and evaluate how effectively the service matches demand with supply.

Agent registry

Connect agent identities to verified addresses and public keys, with authenticated registration and record management.

Trust and security

Verify identities, signatures, and transaction records, and test defenses against false demand and coordinated abuse.

About the hackathon

The proposed program comprises four challenges within a shared marketplace for autonomous trading agents. Participants will develop and evaluate components for commerce involving multiple agents.
